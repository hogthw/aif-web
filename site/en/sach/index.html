Published Q1/2027

Lead Yourself First

Before leading others

You cannot lead others while you are still ruled by yourself. Through concrete exercises on ego, energy, habits and mindset, Lead Yourself First helps you build inner strength, own your choices and act steadily in the AI era. Each chapter is a practical step, from managing emotions to protecting your attention, so you can go further while staying at peace inside.

Format 14 × 20.5 cm · No more than 250 pages · Ho Chi Minh City Economic Publishing House

Published Q1/2027

BodhiMind Leadership

When the tea leaves settle

Amid growth pressure and the turbulence of the AI era, leaders need three things: wisdom to decide, inner strength to stay grounded and compassion to lead. BodhiMind Leadership brings Buddhist wisdom into the very practical work of management: making decisions, building teams, creating value. Through real-life situations, you learn to stand firm in a crisis, resolve conflict and pursue success through responsible choices.

Ho Chi Minh City Economic Publishing House
