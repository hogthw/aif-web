DCS: Protect data with encryption, wherever it goes.

Putting AI into operations without controlling data is leaving the warehouse door open. DCS (data-centric security) encrypts documents as soon as they are created or saved, and attaches a protection policy to each file: who can open, edit, print or share it, and for how long. The protection travels with the file via email, USB or the cloud. DCS logs access and can block sensitive documents from being put into public AI tools.

For: manufacturing, electronics and semiconductor companies with drawings and know-how to protect; finance, banking, healthcare, legal, logistics; any organization holding customer and employee records.

FAQ

How much does implementation cost?

It depends on scope. Every project starts with an assessment and a limited-scope pilot, so you see the value before scaling.

Do GoAI's AI agents make decisions on their own?

Only within the limits you allow. Important decisions always have human approval, and every action is logged.

Is DCS a cybersecurity service?

No. DCS is data security software that uses encryption to protect the data files themselves.
