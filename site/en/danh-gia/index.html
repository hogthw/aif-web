AI readiness is the degree to which an organization has the strategy, data, processes, people and governance for AI to create measurable value. You can measure it at four levels: a province (ARI), an industrial park (ARI-IP™), a company (EAR Index™) and a process (Traffic Light).

What you get

  • Instant results when you finish, no waiting for anyone to send them.
  • A score for each pillar, so you immediately see the weakest point to prioritize.
  • A detailed report by email, if you want one: GoAI experts analyze each pillar and suggest concrete actions. Free of charge. Only then do you need to leave your email.
  • Book a 1-on-1 session with a GoAI expert to turn your results into a plan. Free of charge.

FAQ

What is AI readiness?

It is the degree to which an organization has the strategy, data, processes, people and governance for AI to create measurable value. Having bought AI tools does not mean being ready.

Which tool should I use?

Provinces and cities: ARI. Industrial parks: ARI-IP™. Companies: EAR Index™. To check a single process: Traffic Light.

Is there a fee, do I have to register?

No. The surveys, detailed reports and the results session with an expert are all free. You see your results as soon as you finish, with no need to leave your details.

Are the results made public?

No.

Is this score a certification?

No. It is a diagnostic tool to help leadership make decisions, not a certification or an official ranking.
