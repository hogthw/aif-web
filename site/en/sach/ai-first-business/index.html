Businesses do not need more AI. Businesses need to be rebuilt around AI.

The question in the boardroom is no longer “Does AI matter?”. The question is where to start, what to invest in first, and how to prove the investment pays off. AI First Business answers exactly those three questions, with tools to act on right away.

AI First Business: Rebuilding the enterprise in the AI era. By Trần Tấn Sỹ. Ho Chi Minh City Economic Publishing House.

AI First Business: Rebuilding the enterprise in the AI era is a book by Trần Tấn Sỹ for business leaders. It shows how to move from scattered use of AI tools to rebuilding strategy, organization and processes around AI, using the Adopt – Adapt – Advance framework, with four AI-Native business models, two-tier ROI, AI agent governance, and the EAR Index™ tool with a 90-day roadmap. Published by the Ho Chi Minh City Economic Publishing House.

Most AI investment is being burned on patchwork.

Companies buy tools, bolt them onto old ways of working, then are disappointed when they see no results. Independent studies by RAND, MIT, Gartner and S&P Global all point to the same thing: most enterprise AI projects have not created measurable value, and the cause is not the technology. The cause is the lack of goals, the lack of an accountable owner and the lack of measurement. The book calls this the bolt-on trap, and shows the way out.

The book helps you decide

  • Which process that now takes three days could be cut to a few hours?
  • Which data goes into AI, and which data must stay inside the company?
  • When to use off-the-shelf tools, when to adapt them to your own processes, when to build your own capabilities?
  • Should AI results be measured in productivity, cost, revenue or accumulated assets?
  • As AI agents take on more work, how do the roles of employees, managers and the CEO change?

FAQ

Who is AI First Business for?

For business leaders without a deep technical background who want to make AI investment decisions on a sound basis and with measurement.

Does the book give instructions for specific AI tools?

No. Tools change every quarter. The book focuses on strategy, organization, governance and measurement, the things that decide whether tools make money.

What is the EAR Index™?

A self-assessment tool for enterprise AI readiness across 8 pillars, developed by the author in Chapter 12. See the EAR Index™.

Do I need to read AI Economy first?

Not required. AI Economy gives the national picture; AI First Business is for action inside the company.
