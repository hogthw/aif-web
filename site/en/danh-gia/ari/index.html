AI readiness assessment / ARI

AI investors don't ask what incentives you offer. They ask how ready your province is.

Backup power, computing infrastructure, engineers and the speed of processing applications have become prerequisites. Provinces that answer with data will be chosen. Provinces that answer with tax incentives will be passed over. ARI shows where your province is strong and weak before investors score it themselves.

ARI (AI Readiness Index) is an index proposed by Trần Tấn Sỹ in the book AI Economy, helping a province or city self-assess its capacity to attract AI capital, technology and talent across 8 weighted pillars. Each pillar is scored from 1 to 5; the total score ranges from 1.0 to 5.0.

The country is slipping in the rankings, so each province must speed up.

According to UNCTAD's Frontier Technologies Readiness Index (2024 data), Vietnam ranks 64th of 170, down 11 places from 2022. It ranks 15th in the Finance component but 120th in Skills. Capital may come, but the people to operate AI infrastructure are lacking. The race between provinces will be decided at exactly these bottlenecks.

Eight pillars

The eight ARI pillars
PillarWeightCore criteria
01. Energy and clean energy20%Backup power capacity for large projects; share of renewable energy; grid connection time
02. Computing and AI infrastructure15%Data centers meeting international standards; access to GPUs and cloud computing
03. Connectivity and digital infrastructure10%Fiber density and redundancy; regional connection latency
04. Workforce and talent ecosystem15%Technical and IT training institutions; university–business links
05. Data and governance10%Level of digitization of administrative data; digital one-stop mechanism for investment procedures
06. Capital and startup ecosystem10%Local investment funds or credit guarantees for technology companies
07. Smart infrastructure and next-generation industrial parks15%Share of industrial parks meeting infrastructure standards for AI Factories (per ARI-IP™)
08. Overall ecosystem5%Level of inter-agency coordination in processing high-tech investment applications

How to read the score

Each pillar is scored from 1 (no foundation) to 5 (internationally competitive). The total score is for comparison and tracking progress, not for declaring “ready”. A province scoring high on seven pillars but weak on energy will still lose large AI Factory projects, because without power nothing else is worth discussing.

ARI assessment session with an expert

  1. Agree on the scope and participants from departments and agencies.
  2. Score the 8 pillars independently and cross-check the data.
  3. Analyze score gaps and choose the two pillars to invest in first.
  4. Compare with UNCTAD's independent international index for an objective view.

FAQ

How does ARI differ from the EAR Index™?

ARI measures a province; the EAR Index™ measures a company. Both use a weighted 1–5 scale.

Does ARI rank provinces?

No ranking is published. ARI is for a province to look at itself and track progress over time.

Can the weights be adjusted?

Yes. The book states clearly that they can be adjusted to each province's context.

Is ARI an official index?

No. ARI is a diagnostic tool developed by the author (version 1.0, August 2026), not a ranking or a legal basis.

Sources: AI Economy, Chapter 8 and Appendix A; UNCTAD, Technology and Innovation Report 2025.
